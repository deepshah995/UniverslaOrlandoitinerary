Deep & Nishita's Motion Sickness Golden Rules

Follow these tailored strategies to stay energized and 100% nausea-free throughout the vacation

1. Medication Timing

Take Less-Drowsy Dramamine (Meclizine) or Ginger Chews 45-60 minutes BEFORE arriving at park turnstiles.

2. The 3D Glasses Trick

On simulator rides like Gringotts, simply slide off the 3D glasses during spinning projection screens to instantly stop visual disorientation.

3. Midday Heat Break

Orlando heat peaks from 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM. We have scheduled indoor AC shows (Bourne Stuntacular) and sit-down dining during these peak hours.
