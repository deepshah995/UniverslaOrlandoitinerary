Deep & Nishita's Motion Sickness Golden Rules

Pro strategies to stay energized and 100% nausea-free

1. Medication Timing

Take Less-Drowsy Dramamine or Ginger Chews 45-60 minutes BEFORE arriving at park turnstiles.

2. The 3D Glasses Trick

On Gringotts, simply slide off 3D glasses during spinning projection screens to instantly stop visual disorientation.

3. Midday Heat Break

Orlando heat peaks from 1:00 – 4:00 PM. We scheduled indoor AC shows (Bourne) and sit-down dining during these hours.
